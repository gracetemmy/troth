02 · Five Sibyl tiers, one trust label

Not a database. A living evidence field.

Sibyl Memory ships five tiers. Troth routes each piece of relationship memory into the one where it belongs, then adds a trust label of its own on top: FLAGGED is not a Sibyl tier, it is a status Troth writes onto a WARM entity. Uncertain content stays out of confirmed memory until a human resolves it.

HOT

Active deal, objections, next action.

WARM

Confirmed facts, pricing, signed scope.

COLD

Timestamped calls, emails, decisions.

REFERENCE

Standing pricing and policy rules.

FLAGGED*

Unconfirmed or conflicting promises.

ARCHIVE

Closed relationships and proof.

* FLAGGED is Troth's own status field on Sibyl's WARM tier, not a sixth Sibyl tier. The five above are Sibyl Memory's real schema.

03 · The loop

Ingest once. Verify every commitment.

A transcript, email thread or notes dump becomes structured memory. Nothing bypasses the same audit trail used by manual entries.

01 / INGEST

Drop the conversation.

Troth extracts client facts, policies, timeline entries and promises from the raw material.

02 / CHECK

Test the promise.

Every commitment is checked live against REFERENCE rules before it can become confirmed memory.

03 / RESOLVE

Let a human decide.

Approve, retract or archive a FLAGGED promise. Every resolution is written to COLD.

04 / PROVE

Keep the receipt.

Archive is a state change, not disappearance. Troth can prove who was retired, when, and why.
